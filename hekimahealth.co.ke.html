About Our Clinic

Serving Kajiado County with integrity, excellence, and a deep commitment to community health since 2015.

Our Vision

To be the leading healthcare provider in the Rift Valley region, known for innovation and patient-centered care.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality medical services to the residents of Kajiado, ensuring no one is left behind in the journey to wellness.

Our Specialized Services

We offer a wide range of medical services tailored to the specific needs of our local community.

Maternal Health

Safe delivery, prenatal care, and postnatal support provided by experienced midwives and obstetricians.

Laboratory Testing

Accurate diagnostics including malaria, HIV, typhoid, and full blood counts with fast turnaround times.

Outpatient Care

Daily consultations for malaria, respiratory infections, and chronic disease management like Diabetes/Hypertension.
